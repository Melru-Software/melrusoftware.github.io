From first email to working software.

Short cycles, plain language, and something you can click on early.

  1. Tell us the problem

    Email us what's slow, manual, or missing. We'll ask a few questions and tell you whether software is the right fix.

  2. Agree on a scope

    You get a written scope, a timeline, and a price before anything is built. No surprises later.

  3. See it early, often

    You see a working version early, not at the end. You react, we adjust.

  4. Launch and keep going

    We deploy it, hand over everything, and stay available for the next round of improvements.

Two people who have a low tolerance for inefficiency.

Melru is a husband and wife team. We're both senior software engineers who spent years at large software companies, one of us on the backend and one on the frontend, and we came away with a lot of experience in how big companies build software. AI writes a lot of our code now. What it can't do is tell you whether the code is any good. We can, because we spent years writing it ourselves.

We're problem solvers by habit. When something at work or at home isn't as easy as it should be, one of us ends up building the fix. It's the same habit we bring to your project. We build the smallest thing that fixes it and grow from there.

"Melru" is the name of our street. It's short, easy to say, and it's where we work.
