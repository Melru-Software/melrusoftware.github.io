Why Melru

Two people who have a low tolerance for inefficiency.

Melru is a husband and wife team. We're both senior software engineers who spent years at large software companies, one of us on the backend and one on the frontend, and we came away with a lot of experience in how big companies build software. AI writes a lot of our code now. What it can't do is tell you whether the code is any good. That part is us, and it's the part we spent our careers on.

We're problem solvers by habit. When something at work or at home isn't as easy as it should be, one of us ends up building the fix. That habit is how Melru started, and it's the same thing we bring to your project. We build the smallest thing that fixes it and grow from there.

"Melru" is the name of our street. It's short, easy to say, and it's where we work.
